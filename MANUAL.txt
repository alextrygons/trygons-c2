1. WHAT THIS PROJECT IS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An autopilot for a boat, running on the Infineon KIT_A2G_TC375_LITE board
(AURIX TC375, three CPU cores). There are two variants:

  * SMALL BOAT (default): outboard engine. The software controls the steering
    motor (no angle sensor: the angle is estimated), the throttle servo and
    the gear servo (Forward / Neutral / Reverse). It uses compass, gyro and
    GPS. Driving modes: MANUAL (joystick), HEADING LOCK and AUTO (waypoint
    program). It navigates without GPS (dead reckoning), calibrates itself
    (TRAINING), limits the engine power until you raise it on purpose, and
    logs everything to an SD card.
  * BIG BOAT: rudder with an angle sensor, heading hold only.

The board has not arrived yet. All of the logic is written and tested in a
PC simulation with a simulated boat. The parts that talk to the real
hardware are mostly empty "stubs" for now (see chapter 15).

The three cores:
  core 0  the control loop (20 Hz): modes, steering, engine, PC link
  CPU1    an independent safety monitor (checks core 0 every 1 ms)
  core 2  the logger (writes the SD card)

A PC ground station shows the boat live and sends commands, over the
board's USB cable or to the simulator.

  hal/hal.h       The list of hardware functions the app may use.

  Cpu0_Main.c     core 0: start-up, watchdog, the 20 Hz loop.
                  "#define BOAT_VARIANT_BIG" at the top selects the big boat.
  Cpu1_Main.c     CPU1: the safety monitor loop (1 kHz).
  Cpu2_Main.c     core 2: the logger loop.
  sim/            PC simulation, all tests, simulated boat for the ground

  groundstation/  PC ground station and log tools (Python).

limit) but never stops or starts anything.

7. HOW THE BOAT BEHAVES: MODES, BUTTONS, JOYSTICK
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODES
  STANDBY    engine idle, neutral, steering off. Power-up and after faults.
  MANUAL     the joystick: stick left/right = outboard angle (full range),
             forward/back = throttle ahead/astern (centre = idle + neutral).
             Gear changes always go idle -> neutral -> gear, however fast
             you move the stick.
  HDG LOCK   press HDG LOCK on the panel: the autopilot holds the heading
             the boat has now. -1/+1/-10/+10 (panel or PC) change it. The
             throttle stays on the stick (ahead and astern) - you stay in
             HDG LOCK. In reverse the outboard is held straight. Moving the
             stick sideways takes over (MANUAL).
  AUTO       a waypoint program (up to 50 waypoints). Each waypoint has a
             leg speed, an arrival radius and an action: continue / slow
             down / stop in neutral. At the end: stop + alarm (or hold the
             heading slowly). AUTO starts only with the PC "AUTO START"
             AND the joystick CONFIRM within 10 s (stick centred). Pause,
             resume (+ CONFIRM) and skip from the PC. Line following,
             speed control, rough-water slow-down, dead reckoning without
             GPS, GPS integrity check: all as before.
  TRAINING   outboard calibration, started from STANDBY by the PC.

  Mode changes allowed (all others are refused and logged):
     from \ to   STANDBY MANUAL HDG_LOCK AUTO TRAINING
     STANDBY        -      yes     yes    yes    yes
     MANUAL        yes      -      yes    yes    no
     HDG_LOCK      yes     yes      -     yes    no
     AUTO          yes     yes     yes     -     no
     TRAINING      yes     yes     no     no      -
  Extra rules: only the joystick enters MANUAL; MANUAL is left only with
  the stick centred; AUTO needs compass, GPS, a valid program and CONFIRM;
  TRAINING only from STANDBY by the PC; nothing but STANDBY until the
  outputs are armed (chapter 10).

THE JOYSTICK ALWAYS WINS
  * Take over from ANY mode: stick sideways past 15 % for 0.1 s, forward /
    back (not in HDG LOCK, where that is the throttle), or TAKE. Chirp.
    An AUTO program is suspended (resume later: PC + CONFIRM).
  * Give back: CENTRE the stick, then joystick AUTO (back to HDG LOCK if
    that was interrupted, otherwise STANDBY) or panel HDG LOCK. Not
    centred: refused + chirp. The PC can't take control from the stick.
  * CONFIRM only answers a request from the PC (AUTO start / resume, power
    raise). It counts only with the stick centred and the joystick armed.
    Nothing pending: nothing happens. It never changes the mode by itself.
  * At power-up the stick must be centred, otherwise it is ignored and the
    outputs stay off (alarm) until you let go.
  * Broken wire while it drives: idle, neutral, steering stops, STANDBY.

THE STANDBY BUTTON ALWAYS WINS
  Every mode -> STANDBY (idle + neutral first, then steering off). If the
  stick is still held, it is ignored until centred. The PC can't override
  it. In STANDBY the button acknowledges alarms.

AUTO PROGRAM - HOW TO
  1. Program tab: add waypoints (or tick "click adds" and click the map),
     set leg speed, radius, action; choose the end action.
  2. "Upload to boat". The boat checks it (coordinates, number <= 50, leg
     length 10 m .. 50 km, speeds within the maximum, radius 5..500 m) and
     stores it with a checksum (kept after power-off).
  3. Raise the power limit if you want more than ~7 kn (chapter 8).
  4. AUTO START, then CONFIRM on the joystick within 10 s.

8. THE POWER LIMIT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * After EVERY power-up or reset: 30 % forward, 5 % reverse (of the full
    throttle travel). Never saved.
  * Raise, one step at a time (30 -> 50 -> 75 -> 100 %; reverse 5 -> 10 -> 15):
      1. "Raise fwd" (or "Raise rev") in the ground station,
      2. type the 4-digit code the window shows, "Send code",
      3. press CONFIRM on the joystick within 10 s (stick centred).
    Each step needs the whole sequence again. Wrong / old code: refused.
  * It drops back to 30 / 5 AT ONCE on: "Lower now", TAKE or a stick
    takeover, the STANDBY button, ANY alarm (incl. PC link lost, GPS lost,
    compass / joystick / gear fault), entering TRAINING, and after 2 min
    below 3 kn. The throttle then comes down at its normal rate (no sudden
    cut), but it can never go above the new limit.
  * In AUTO the leg speed is limited by it; the window shows "SPEED LIMITED
    BY POWER LIMIT".
  * Enforced in the lowest layer: app/servo.c, the only code that drives
    the throttle servo. The CPU1 monitor checks it again.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. ALARMS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  COMPASS LOST, GPS LOST, GPS SUSPECT, DEAD RECKONING LIMIT,
  ARRIVAL UNCERTAIN, ARRIVED, GEAR FAULT, TRAINING,
  JOYSTICK FAULT, JOYSTICK NOT CENTRED, JOYSTICK TOOK CONTROL,
  PC LINK LOST
  NOT NEUTRAL AT START   outputs not armed: gear not in neutral / stick held
  SAFETY MONITOR TRIPPED CPU1 forced idle + neutral (see chapter 10)
  CONTROL LOOP OVERRUN   a control cycle took longer than 50 ms
  LOG FAILURE            SD card missing / full / failing (control goes on)

  Every new alarm drops the power limit to 30 / 5 and starts a black-box
  file. Acknowledge: STANDBY button in STANDBY, or "ACK ALARM".


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. SAFETY RULES BUILT INTO THE SOFTWARE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Gear changes only at idle: idle -> wait -> neutral -> wait -> gear.
  * Every fault / STANDBY: idle + neutral FIRST, then steering off.
    Compass lost while moving (AUTO / TRAINING): gyro steering until
    stopped (or outboard to centre if the gyro is gone too). In HDG LOCK:
    gyro hold, the throttle stays with you; STANDBY once you have stopped.
    In MANUAL: alarm only.
  * Throttle rate limits; reverse limited; the power limit (chapter 8).
  * The joystick always wins; the STANDBY button always wins.
  * NEUTRAL-START INTERLOCK: after power-up the outputs only arm when the
    throttle is idle, neutral is confirmed (neutral switch if fitted) and
    the stick is centred. Until then: STANDBY only, safety relay open,
    alarm after 3 s.
  * WATCHDOGS on all three cores (100 ms on core 0). A stuck loop resets
    the chip: everything starts again in STANDBY at 30 %.
  * CPU1 SAFETY MONITOR: checks every 1 ms, on its own: throttle above the
    power limit, gear moving with the throttle open, core 0 silent for
    200 ms. Then it forces idle + neutral + steering off + alarm + safety
    relay open, and core 0 can't undo it until a reset. Reaction in the
    simulator: 10-20 ms (one check), 220 ms for a frozen core 0.
  * Heading hold pauses (outboard held still) when there is no propulsion,
    so the angle estimate can't drift while you are stopped.
  * Logging can never block control (chapter 11).

11. LOGGING (SD CARD) AND THE LOG TOOLS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS LOGGED: 20 times a second 86 values (mode, all sensors raw and
filtered, GPS with satellites and spoof flag, PID terms, outboard estimate,
throttle after every limit stage, gear, power limit, navigation, position
estimate + uncertainty, current, joystick, buttons, CPU times, supply
voltage) and every event with its time (mode changes and refused requests,
every PC command with its answer, every button, alarms on/off, power-limit
changes and raise attempts, training, GPS lost / suspect / recovered,
faults, resets with the reason, program steps, config changes).

FILES on the card (FAT32, read them with a card reader):
  Bbbbbb_fff_YYYYMMDD-HHMM.TLG   b = boot number, f = file number; a new
                                 file every 10 min (date once GPS is fixed)
  BBbbbbb_nnn.TLG                black box: the 60 s before an alarm /
                                 fault and the 30 s after
Every file starts with the description of the fields and the full config,
so every file can be read on its own. A power cut loses at most ~2 s.

THE TOOLS (in groundstation\, use C:\msys64\ucrt64\bin\python.exe):
    python logview.py FILE.TLG          plots, map, events; click an event
                                        to jump there
    python log2csv.py FILE.TLG          -> CSV for Excel (all fields)
    python log2csv.py FILE.TLG -f heading,target,thr_out --events
    python log2csv.py FILE.TLG --list   the field names
    python tlgreplay.py FILE.TLG        replay the drive in the simulator
The ground station saves its own copy of the telemetry in the same format
(groundstation\logs\log_*.TLG).

15. WHAT IS STILL MISSING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A) THE REAL HARDWARE FUNCTIONS (hal/hal_tc375.c)
   Written: timer (ms / us), alarm LED, safe pin states at start-up,
   watchdogs, reset reason, monitor latch, memory barrier.
   Still stubs:
     [ ] compass, gyro / IMU (+ roll), GPS (NMEA + date + satellites)
     [ ] steering motor driver PWM + direction, motor current (ADC)
     [ ] throttle servo PWM and gear servo PWM (GTM, 50 Hz) - including the
         direct idle / neutral pulses in hal_safe_outputs()
     [ ] safety relay output (pin done, relay hardware missing)
     [ ] neutral switch, limit switches, paddlewheel, supply voltage
     [ ] panel buttons (6 inputs + debounce), joystick ADC + 3 buttons
     [ ] flash storage (TRAINING result, joystick calibration, AUTO
         program, boot counter)
     [ ] USB link to the PC (ASCLIN0, 921600 baud)
     [ ] microSD card: QSPI2 driver + FatFs (with long file names)
     [ ] rudder angle sensor (big boat only)

B) TO CHECK ON THE BOARD
     [ ] The watchdog timeout (100 ms) and that a watchdog timeout really
         resets the chip (SMU alarm configuration).
     [ ] That the black box ends up in CPU1's RAM with TASKING (the pragma
         is there; check the map with tools\mapreport.py).
     [ ] Real CPU times (System tab): the PC needs 0.5 us per control step,
         the TC375 with a -O0 debug build will need much more; the budget
         is 50 ms.
     [ ] The QSPI2 / mikroBUS pin functions and the SD card speed.

C) TO DECIDE / CHECK ON THE BOAT
     [ ] Pin plan for compass, GPS, IMU (only the joystick, panel, servos,
         relay, neutral switch and SD card pins are chosen).
     [ ] Calibrate the throttle servo endpoints (idle / full pulse) and the
         gear pulses; fit the throttle return spring; wire the safety relay.
     [ ] Measure the outboard (TRAINING), tune the steering on the water.
     [ ] Try the power-limit steps and the low-speed drop at sea.

D) KNOWN LIMITATIONS / JUDGEMENT CALLS
     * After a power-limit drop the throttle comes down at 40 %/s (not a
       sudden cut), which takes up to ~1.8 s from 100 % to 30 %.
     * The STANDBY button works even with the stick held (the stick is
       then ignored until centred). The PC can't leave MANUAL at all.
     * A STOP waypoint only chirps; the "ARRIVED" alarm is for the end.
     * The PC may acknowledge alarms in any mode (the panel button only in
       STANDBY).
     * A normally-open STANDBY button can't be pressed if its wire breaks;
       a normally-closed button with a line check would catch that.
     * Settings changed from the PC are lost at power-off.
     * A new program can't be uploaded while AUTO runs.
     * Joystick hand-back in a running AUTO goes to STANDBY (resume = PC +
       CONFIRM).

16. TROUBLESHOOTING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  "gcc is not recognized"        C:\msys64\ucrt64\bin is not in PATH.
  "python" opens the Store       use C:\msys64\ucrt64\bin\python.exe
  BUILD FAILED                   read the first error line (file + line).
  Ground station "Cannot connect" is "autopilot_sim.exe server" running?
  "already in use?"              another simulator runs: close it or use
                                 another port (server 5770).
  "JOYSTICK IS IN CONTROL"       on purpose: centre the stick, press AUTO.
  AUTO START does nothing        press CONFIRM on the joystick within 10 s
                                 (stick centred); is a program uploaded?
  Power raise refused            acknowledge alarms first; type the code
                                 shown now; then CONFIRM within 10 s.
  The boat is slow in AUTO       the power limit: raise it (chapter 8).
  "NOT NEUTRAL AT START"         put the gear in neutral / let go of the
                                 stick; check the neutral switch.
  "LOG FAILURE"                  SD card missing, full or broken. Control
                                 is not affected.
